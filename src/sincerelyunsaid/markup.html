Why this exists

It's the thing that echoes in your head.

That you rehearse in the shower.

That you say to yourself so often that it seems crazy other people don't already know.

Somewhere along the way, I noticed how much of this goes unsaid.

Telling someone else's story has always come easier than telling your own.

So I made a room where the quiet ones go first. We begin in the middle, where the real part already started. Then, we learn how.

If you have something you have been carrying, there is a space for it here.

Sincerely, S. Afrin
